# Code of conduct

Altera is a small project built by and for students. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests or anywhere else connected to the project, is
expected to help keep it a friendly place to contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision on scope even
  when you disagree.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never post someone else's name, student
  number, grades or messages, including in screenshots.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to get around CourseLink's or the University of Guelph's
  rules, or to access anyone else's account or data.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately,
use the "Report content" option on the comment, or open a private security
advisory from the repository's Security tab if it can't be raised in public.
